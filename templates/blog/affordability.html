How Much Mortgage Can I Afford in the UK?

Simple UK affordability guide • First-time buyers & movers

One of the first questions people ask is: “How much can I actually borrow?” The answer depends on your income, deposit, debts, and the lender’s criteria.

1. Income Multiples (The Quick Rule of Thumb)

Most UK lenders will lend somewhere around:

  • 4x to 4.5x your annual income as a starting point
  • Sometimes up to 5x or 5.5x for high earners with low debts

Example: If you earn £50,000 per year, a typical max range might be: £200,000–£250,000 mortgage.

2. Your Deposit & Loan-to-Value (LTV)

Your deposit percentage changes everything:

  • 5–10% deposit → higher rates, fewer lenders
  • 15–25% deposit → much better choice & rates
  • 40%+ deposit → often the cheapest deals

3. Monthly Payment Comfort Zone

Forget the maximum the bank will lend for a moment. Ask yourself: “What monthly payment can I comfortably afford?”

A good rule is to aim for your total mortgage payment to be no more than 25–30% of your take-home pay where possible.
