UK mortgage rates have been moving a lot in 2024–2025. The good news? if you compare properly, the difference between an “okay” deal and a “great” deal can easily be worth £3,000–£8,000 over the initial fixed period.

What Counts as a Good Mortgage Rate in 2025?

For strong-credit borrowers with a decent deposit (20%+), a competitive 2 or 5 year fixed rate in late 2025 typically looks like:

  • 2 year fixed: around 4–5% APR
  • 5 year fixed: around 4–4.8% APR
  • 10 year fixed: slightly higher, but more stability

Why Fees Matter More Than You Think

Many “best buy” tables show the lowest rate — but hide the fact that the deal comes with a £999 or £1,499 product fee. Once you add that fee on top, a slightly higher rate with no fee can actually be cheaper.

That’s why MortgageDealsHub focuses on true cost: monthly payment + fees – cashback over the fixed period.
