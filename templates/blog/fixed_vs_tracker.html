Fixed vs Tracker Mortgages – Which Is Better?

Understand your options before you lock in

One of the biggest mortgage decisions you’ll make is fixed rate vs tracker. Both have pros and cons, and the right choice depends on how much certainty you want.

What Is a Fixed-Rate Mortgage?

A fixed-rate mortgage keeps your interest rate the same for a set period (e.g. 2, 3, 5 or 10 years). Your monthly payment won’t change during that time.

  • ✅ Certainty – you always know your payment
  • ✅ Good if rates are expected to rise
  • ❌ Usually early repayment charges if you leave early

What Is a Tracker Mortgage?

A tracker mortgage moves in line with a base rate (often the Bank of England base rate), plus a set percentage.

  • ✅ Can be cheaper if rates fall
  • ✅ Sometimes lower fees or more flexibility
  • ❌ Payments can rise if interest rates go up

Which One Is Right for You?

Choose a fixed-rate mortgage if you:

  • Like predictable payments
  • Have a tight budget
  • Think rates might rise

Consider a tracker if you:

  • Can handle payments moving up and down
  • Think rates might fall or stay low
  • Want more flexibility in some products
