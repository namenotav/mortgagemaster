❓ Frequently Asked Questions

✅ Is MortgageDealsHub free to use?

Yes! You can search 30,000+ mortgage deals and run calculations 100% free forever. No credit card required. Our optional Premium (£19.99/month) or Premium+ (£49.99/month) plans add extra tools like unlimited deal tracking, rate change alerts, and advanced calculators.

🏦 Are you a mortgage broker?

No. We're an independent comparison and education platform. We don't give regulated financial advice or sell mortgages. We simply help you find and compare deals. You should always speak to an FCA-regulated adviser before making a final mortgage decision.

📊 Does using this affect my credit score?

No, never! We don't run credit checks. We simply help you understand and compare the deals that might be suitable for you based on your situation. Only the lender will check your credit when you apply.

🔍 How many deals do you have?

We have 30,000+ live mortgage deals from 1,000+ UK lenders — the LARGEST database in the UK. This includes mainstream banks (HSBC, Barclays, Nationwide), building societies, and specialist lenders for bad credit, low income, self-employed, and unique situations.

❤️ Do you help people with bad credit?

Yes! We're one of the few sites that actively helps people with credit scores as low as 300, CCJs, defaults, IVAs, and other adverse credit. We have specialist lenders who WANT to work with you. Don't give up — we can help!

💰 How do you make money?

We earn revenue from our PRO subscription plan and, in the future, may earn from introductions to trusted FCA-regulated mortgage brokers. This never changes how we rank or display deals — we always show you the best matches for YOUR situation, not ours.
