Frequently Asked Questions

Is MortgageDealsHub free to use?

Yes. You can compare deals and run calculations for free. Our optional Pro plan adds extra tools like unlimited tracked deals and rate alerts.

Are you a mortgage broker?

No. We’re an independent comparison and education tool. We don’t give regulated financial advice. You should always speak to an FCA-regulated adviser before making a final decision.

Does using this affect my credit score?

No. We don’t run credit checks. We simply help you understand and compare the deals that might be suitable for you.

How do you make money?

We may earn money from our Pro plan and, in future, from introductions to trusted FCA-regulated brokers. This never changes how we rank deals for you.
