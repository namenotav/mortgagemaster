📋 What Counts as "Bad Credit"?

Minor Issues (Score 550-650)

  • 1-2 missed payments on utilities/phone
  • High credit card balances (over 50% utilization)
  • Overdraft regularly maxed out
  • Short credit history (under 3 years)

Impact: May still get mainstream lender with higher rate

Moderate Issues (Score 400-550)

  • CCJs (County Court Judgments) - small amounts under £1,000
  • Defaults on credit cards/loans (2+ years ago)
  • Multiple missed payments
  • Debt management plan (completed)

Impact: Need specialist lender, 1-2% higher rate

Severe Issues (Score 300-400)

  • Multiple CCJs or large CCJs (£2,000+)
  • IVA (Individual Voluntary Arrangement)
  • Bankruptcy (discharged)
  • Repossession
  • Debt relief order

Impact: Together Money, Pepper Money - expect 2-3% higher rate
